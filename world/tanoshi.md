# Tanoshi — a type of yokai

**Status:** Author-confirmed, October 14, 2026. **Source:** Phil's explicit canon request, recorded in [author-confirmed canon](../canon/author-confirmed.md#tanoshi--october-14-2026).

## Type and individuals

**Tanoshi** is the exact name of a **type of yokai**, rather than a single unique being. Many Tanoshi individuals appear in different places. Individuals can share one master visual design while differing in **colors, facial expressions and personalities**.

This supersedes a unique-being interpretation of the name. No individual names, population count, powers, origins, rarity, cosmological relationships or seven-age placement have been supplied.

## Approved visual reference

The **approved Tanoshi avatar** is the visual reference for the shared master design. Its final atlas is preserved in the **Critz-Tycoon** repository at `references/tanoshi/originals/Tanoshi-spritesheet.png`; `references/tanoshi/README.md` and `references/tanoshi/manifest.json` record its reference history and provenance. The earlier static preview is not the final atlas.

The final atlas's SHA-256 is `455e8a6a5664e8cf4104b9a9c5872d03b77cbc00126be09cddc6a5beb46f4701`. Pet animation states and technical asset dimensions do not establish fictional abilities or biology. A game adaptation's individual colors, expressions and personalities are design choices within Phil's approved variation, not additional author-confirmed biographies.

## Critz Tycoon

Players can discover **different Tanoshi hiding around the world**. These are different individuals of the same yokai type, with the approved avatar as the common visual reference. See the [game registry entry](../games/registry.md#critz-tycoon-tanoshi-discovery).

Exact hiding places, discovery counts, rewards and mechanics remain implementation decisions. This request does not assign Critz Tycoon or Tanoshi to an age.

## Eutychus and the avatar

**Eutychus is Phil's AI dot.** The little **Tanoshi is its avatar**, depicting the fictional yokai type. Eutychus and the assistant are not literally yokai. **Uticus** is a voice-dictation variant of **Eutychus**, not an approved rename.
