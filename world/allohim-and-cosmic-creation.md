# Allohim, the universe, and created worlds

**Status:** Author-confirmed cosmic setting, October 11, 2026. Distinguish the author's claims *within fiction* from real-world astronomy or theology.

## Allohim

**Allohim** creates **the entire universe**, including its many galaxies, worlds, and creative beings. He does not personally design most worlds' particular environments: instead, he gives **different numbers of created designers with creative power** to different worlds. These designer beings shape their entrusted worlds.

**Libriam** is the official name for **the entire planet** containing Hokkaido and two other great continents. **Dragex and Moana** are the two creative beings commissioned for **Libriam**. They create the things of that world. The older April 2026 outline describes a land/sea division and jointly-created birds; keep that as recovered explanatory detail unless separately corrected, rather than inventing new species-by-species creation assignments.

**Earth** is the one world **Allohim personally designed**. It lies in the **Milky Way galaxy** and is so remote from the other designer-built worlds that **they will never make contact with it**.

### Important boundaries

- There are multiple galaxies in this fictional universe, not just one.
- Number of galaxies, their names beyond the Milky Way, total number of worlds, creative-beings-per-world counts, star systems, travel speeds, and age-of-universe chronology are not specified.
- Nothing establishes that Earth humans originally sailed to Hokkaido. In the latest account Allohim directly places the first local man on Eden's Isle in Age 6.
- **Libriam** is now the confirmed planet name; **Hokkaido** is a continent within it. “Libriam” is an author-created word, not an attested Latin geographic noun. A suggested connection to Latin *libra* (balance/scale) is thematic, not a rigorous translation.
- **Earth remains inaccessible**: the established cosmic separation says that Earth and other worlds never have contact. **In Age 7**, perfected Libriam develops space exploration and sends inhabitants to help **other reachable created worlds** overcome sin and learn to know and obey Allohim. This does not establish contact with Earth.
- The previous April 2026 lore says Dragex attacked Moana, who left the Tear, and that Dragex slept in an obsidian volcano; the current author has explicitly fixed the sleeping place as **under Mount Yokai**. The precise relationship to the older *Mount Yotei* label remains a historical inconsistency.

## Geographic context

The Hokkaido world also has **two further major continents**, discovered by critters in **Age 4**. The voyages of critters never return; the reason is not established, while some birds travel to and fro and great sea creatures have viewed these lands. Do not confuse these two continents with **Earth**, which is beyond contact.

## Final history of Libriam

During **Age 6 (Man)**, Allohim charges mankind, both men and women, to **fill and subdue the planet**. The task covers all three great continents. Mankind repeatedly fails and fights enemies, including mutated remnants of Fallout and entities called ancient Fey; with Allohim's essential help, humanity eventually conquers all of Libriam, defeats the **last enemy**, and ends sin and death on the world.

The instant that final enemy is defeated begins **Age 7, everlasting Peace/Perfection**. Every creature lives in perfect relationship with Allohim and obeys his will; the **Kingdom of Allohim descends to Libriam**. Allohim promises that sin can never return to Libriam. Mankind rules and harnesses the planet's resources to explore reachable planets, assisting other worlds still fighting sin. Details of starships, technology, specific worlds and campaigns are undecided.

## Grandfather Tree and Fey departure — October 19, 2026

Phil’s latest update places the archive/school at the Creation-planted Grandfather Tree deep in Grimroot Grove. Faithful good Fey converted it during their rule; its former custodians left under Allohim's command for all Fey to sail to Zealand. Rebels stayed and built the very tall Tower of Babel near Sapporo; Allohim leveled it. Subsequent communication difficulties and power struggles led toward Mount Yokai. This Fey voyage precedes the established Age-4 discovery of other continents **by Hokkaido's critters**; the accounts concern different travelers. Fey survival outside the battle remains canon. Naboo took over the archive shortly afterward. See [Grandfather Tree](grandfather-tree.md) for the full account, unresolved near-destruction timing and Naboo terminology.
