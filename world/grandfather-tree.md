# Grandfather Tree, Grimroot Grove and Naboo

**Source:** Phil’s explicit author update, recorded October 19, 2026. This supersedes older conflicting current summaries; recovered manuscripts remain historical evidence.

## Archive and school

The **Grandfather Tree** is massive and was planted during the **Age of Creation**. Hidden deep within **Grimroot Grove**, it houses an enormous archive of knowledge, a museum, a massive library and a collection of extraordinary artifacts. Faithful good Fey converted it into an archive during Fey rule and recorded knowledge there. It is also the site of the school/academy attended by Olivia and by the player in *Critz: Treetop Trebuchet*. The institution's formal name remains undecided; “Great Library” remains an established descriptive name.

The archive was **nearly destroyed**. The cause and exact date are not supplied, nor is its timing relative to the departure, tower or battle established. Its former Fey custodians departed under Allohim's command.

## Fey departure and rebellion

Allohim commanded **all Fey to sail to Zealand**. Rebel Fey stayed and built a **very tall Tower of Babel near Sapporo** to wage war against Allohim. Allohim destroyed and leveled the tower to rubble. Afterward, the Fey had trouble communicating with one another, fought for power, and this led toward the **Battle of Mount Yokai**.

This expands the Age-2 account rather than changing its boundary: the death of the last Fey still fighting at Mount Yokai ends the age. It does **not** imply the death of all Fey everywhere. Fey elsewhere, abstainers and young/newborn Fey survive. No exact tower date, dimensions or mechanism of the communication difficulty is established.

## Naboo

Shortly after the Battle of Mount Yokai, **Naboo**, a **first-generation owl who drank from the River of Blood**, took over the archive and runs the library.

**Author clarification, October 19, 2026:** A **fey animal** is an animal/critter with humanlike qualities because of the **fey blood inside it**. This describes a Fey-blooded critter, not an original Fey being. The spellings **feysian / feyjian** remain unsettled; neither is selected as canonical. Calling Naboo a **fey owl** is therefore consistent with his being a first-generation owl who drank from the River of Blood. This resolves the former classification question. His relationship to the older owl **Lady Wisdom** remains unresolved; do not silently merge them.

## Creative references

The Jedi archive on Coruscant inspires the scale and knowledge collection. A Mirkwood-like confusing, strange forest that affects travelers' minds inspires Grimroot Grove. These are **creative references**, not crossover inhabitants, geography or history.

## Game connection

*Critz: Treetop Trebuchet* occurs in the **early Fourth Age / Age of Arcane**. The critter player's academy project is a trebuchet that launches objects as far as possible, starting with the most basic trebuchet and progressively upgrading. See the [game registry](../games/registry.md#critz-treetop-trebuchet) for the exact early loop and open mechanics, and the [game repository](https://github.com/FreeMarketWildlife/critz-treetop-trebuchet).

## Superseded interpretations and open questions

The current Grandfather Tree is the archive/school in Grimroot Grove. Older Rootport/Woodland Inn tree accounts remain version-specific evidence, not an additional current identity. The proposed lake library is not established as this archive. The school's formal name, Naboo–Lady Wisdom identity, the spelling feysian / feyjian, and the archive's near-destruction cause/date remain open.
