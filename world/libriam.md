# Libriam — the planet

**Status:** Canonically named by the author on October 12, 2026. Spelling: **L-I-B-R-I-A-M**.

## Planet and geography

**Libriam** is the entire world on which the Dragex book series and Critz games occur. **Hokkaido** is only one of its **three major landmasses**, with a few small nearby islands; there are **two other great landmasses**, first discovered by Hokkaido's critters in Age 4. Their explorers never return, for unknown reasons. Birds (hosts of heaven) travel between the lands; certain great ocean creatures have seen them.

The planet is much smaller than Earth. Hokkaido roughly resembles real-world Hokkaido in outline and size, and, under the author’s October 12 scale decision, occupies **83,422.22 km²**, exactly **5% of Libriam’s 1,668,444.4 km² surface**. There is no Honshu-like country south of Hokkaido. Other continent names, surface maps and geology remain open. Assuming a spherical model, the specified area gives a radius of **364.38 km**, diameter of **728.75 km**, and circumference of **2,289.45 km**. These derived dimensions supersede the previous unspecified planetary size; they do not establish physical mass or gravity. See [Dragex Globe](../dragex-globe/README.md).

## Name and linguistic caution

**Libriam** is an original name coined by the author. A screenshot of an earlier AI answer suggested a balance-theme derivation from Latin *libra* (scales/weight), and interpreted Libriam as “realm held in equilibrium.” The **thematic association is a possible artistic reading, not a defensible literal Latin translation**; *-iam* is not a reliable Latin place-name suffix yielding that definition. Neither the planet's in-universe naming language nor its historical etymology has been specified. Do not turn the prior assistant's speculative morphology into settled lore.

## History

- **Age 1 Creation:** Allohim creates the universe and commissions Dragex and Moana to shape Libriam.
- **Age 2 Fall:** Fey stewardship, a near-total catastrophe among those fighting at Mount Yokai, and the collapse of Fey civilization. Surviving Fey remain outside that battle, including young Fey and populations on the two other continents.
- **Age 3 Redemptive Opportunity:** Koric and Veratus return Moana's Tear to Death's Valley on Hokkaido.
- **Age 4 Arcane:** Veratus's Kingdom of Moana, rapid magical technology, discovery of two distant continents, and catastrophic arcane war.
- **Age 5 Fallout:** devastation, disease, zombies/mutations and weakening inherited Fey blood. Ends when Allohim judges and cleanses Hokkaido.
- **Age 6 Man:** a man and woman are placed on Eden's Isle; humanity's mission is to **fill and subdue all of Libriam**, including the three continents. They fail repeatedly, confront mutated creatures and enemies described as ancient Fey, and need Allohim's intervention and support to succeed. Ancient Fey are possible because the Mount Yokai disaster did not exterminate Fey worldwide; their specific identities and allegiances are still unestablished.
- **Age 7 Peace / Perfection:** begins **at the instant the last enemy on Libriam is defeated** and all its creatures submit to Allohim. Sin and death are definitively abolished. **Allohim's Kingdom descends to Libriam**, and he promises that no sin will ever return. Humanity rules Libriam in perfect balance and everlasting peace, develops resources and technology for **space exploration**, and helps inhabitants of **other created planets** struggling with sin to know and obey Allohim.

## Interplanetary boundaries

Allohim designed Earth personally, in the Milky Way, while other worlds were shaped by appointed creative beings. Prior canon says **Earth is so remote from those worlds that they never contact it**. Age-7 journeys thus concern other reachable worlds, **not Earth**, until and unless the author explicitly revises this restriction. Libriam's own sinlessness does not imply other planets are already perfected.

## Globe geography design — October 12, 2026

Hokkaido’s great freshwater lake / later Death’s Valley is anchored at **19.75° N, 9.98° E**. Three rivers drain outward in Ages 1–2; theft of Moana’s Tear near the Age-2/3 transition dries the lake and causes an expanding Age-3 drought. A reference edge is **17.69° N, 1.33° E**. The western mountain range runs **9.97° N, 8.18° W → 2.10° N, 0.79° W**, slowing western drying relative to the east. See the dated [author account](../canon/author-confirmed.md) for attribution and unresolved details.

The author requests draft shapes for the other two landmasses near **24° S, 124° E** and **46° N, 96° W**. The Globe program proposes **1.1%** and **0.9%** of Libriam’s surface respectively. These draft outlines and sizes are an implementation proposal under a conservative combined 2% cap; the names, cultures, histories, mountain heights, and detailed geography remain undecided. Their depiction in earlier ages does not change the confirmed Age-4 discovery by Hokkaido’s critters.

## Revised continents and polar waters — later October 12, 2026

The latest author request replaces the two small procedural shapes. **Alaska**, the named third landmass, is modeled on a rotated Alaska outline, enlarged to **5–6%** of Libriam, and moved west. The app proposes **5.5%**, a **65° rotation**, and a center at **43° N, 140° W**. The second landmass is modeled on rotated **New Zealand** at **6–7%**, stretching toward the south pole; the app proposes **6.5%**, **15° rotation**, and **25° S, 124° E**, with a coastline near **85° S**. These exact implementation values remain design choices within the requested ranges. “New Zealand” is a working reference label, whereas “Alaska” is explicitly author-named.

The north and south poles are **mostly ice-covered ocean**, with land possible but unspecified. The renderer supplies pack ice and broken marginal floes over the mapped surface; it does not add polar continents. This supersedes the earlier 1.1% / 0.9% proposals and combined 2% limit. Hokkaido remains **5%** at **83,422.22 km²**.

## Great rivers and the small lake island — latest October 12, 2026 decision

The author specifies three rivers flowing from the lake at **19.75° N, 9.98° E** to these exact ocean outlets:

- **11.37192° N, 31.43740° E**.
- **14.78480° N, 18.33008° W** (longitude **−18.33008**).
- **36.54197° N, 13.66136° W** (longitude **−13.66136**).

The routes should bend naturally. These endpoints supersede the earlier automatically chosen outlet directions; intermediate bends and widths remain visualization proposals. The current coastline puts the supplied outlets a short distance offshore; the app preserves the exact coordinates with channels meeting the ocean there.

A small island inside the lake has opposite edge points **19.95031° N, 9.15032° E** and **19.25278° N, 11.37802° E**. Their derived great-circle separation on spherical Libriam is **about 14.064 km**. The author requests a pool inside this island, **about 1 km wide**. The app proposes a narrow irregular outline, approximately **3.2 km across**, and a central pool near **19.601545° N, 10.26417° E**. Only the supplied edge anchors and approximate pool size are confirmed; the width, outline and pool center are design choices.

The accompanying **Age 7 / design unit 6999** describes the supplied map-coordinate context; it does not establish that the island first forms in Age 7. The visualization keeps the island in place, gives its pool water during the existing lake's wet periods, and dries it with the existing drought. That earlier pool history is a proposal, not a new author-confirmed event. This island is not identified as Eden's Isle.
