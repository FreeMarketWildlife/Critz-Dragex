## Names and rejected duplicates

- **Critz Valley** is not an eleventh current title: that concept was folded into / identified with **Critz Tycoon**.
- Earlier **Critz Arena** naming for the MOBA was replaced by **Critz Kingdom Conquest**; “Arena” is used in the fighter's **Critz Duel Arena** name.
- **Stacker** is the earlier concept/name behind **Critz Tower Defense**, not another active row.
- **Critz Kingdoms** was renamed **Critz Empire**.
- **Critz Dungeons, Critz Survival, Critz Lab, and Critz Mystery** were rejected suggestions in that planning exchange. Preserve them only as rejected concepts.

## Important separation from the Drive material

Most 2025–2026 `[Critz]` Drive files describe the original shooter. Their modes are **Conquest**, **Duel**, **Blitz**, **Apex**, and **Hide & Hunt**, plus the **Eastlake** lobby/minigames. These are not five additional standalone games. The shooter's Duel / high-stakes Duel Arena references should not automatically be equated with the newer standalone platform fighter.

The October 2025 file titled `[Critz] Campaign` describes a **multi-era mission sequence**, whereas the October 2026 conversation describes a **standalone open-world concept**. Keep both records; their final relationship has not been settled here.

The Kingdom Conquest game's appearances of Dragex and Moana as objectives do not by themselves establish that both creator-beasts are alive together during its literal historical era. No age placement should be inferred from boss selection.
