# Hokkaido — Geography and timeline anchors

Status: Author-confirmed, 2026-10-11. Map labels are transcribed from author-supplied annotated reference; unclear spelling must be confirmed before being treated as canon.

## Geography
- Hokkaido is a landmass resembling the real-world Japanese island, with a few surrounding islands.
- There is no larger Japan or other landmass immediately south of Hokkaido.
- Hokkaido is on a much smaller world than Earth; author estimates Hokkaido covers about **5% of the world's surface** (rough worldbuilding scale, not a precise geodesic measurement).
- Hokkaido has **no known humans during the Dragex novels**.
- Humans eventually arrive by sea from a faraway western land, approximately in the **Sixth Age**, ages after the novel's opening.

## Chronology
- Dragex/Critz lore is organized into **seven ages**.
- **Moana's Tear** begins at the **start of the Fourth Age**.
- Human arrival in Hokkaido is **approximately Sixth Age**, not contemporary with the novel.
- Names, boundaries, and events of the remaining ages have not yet been verified.

## Older lore recovered from past conversations — historical, not automatically current canon
A March 2023 discussion described Dragex and Moana as creator-authorized sibling epic beasts: Dragex formed Hokkaido's land and Mount Yokai; Moana formed oceans, rivers, lakes, and the central sea. Dragex tore out Moana's eye; Moana died, leaving a tear that preserved the central sea. Spirit blood from a Battle of Spirits produced Fey-Blooded/Feysians. In that version Koric stole the Tear, drying the sea into Death's Valley and receiving a reincarnation curse until its return. This is an important historical account **awaiting author's confirmation**. A 2024 account described Korok the Raven Lord and a Phoenix Curse with a thousand-year cycle; also unconfirmed.
