## 2022 outline — detailed plot and setting
Source: [Outline (Jan–Feb 2022)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1l1BTv2psAfx4_slHMfWm1112G426JpaRCoHuUPCFcwk/edit)

- Time: **March 16, 17 ADF (After Dragex Fell)**. Veratus turns **16**.
- **Veratus** is a basilisk lizard, taken in as an egg and raised by **Uncle Roger** and **Aunt Rose**. (A separate 2024 source calls his aunt **Claire**: name conflict.)
- Prologue: **Koric** arrives at **Kuriku Port** and receives an offer from the **Kalos Guild**: bring the last basilisk in Hokkaido, living in Rootport, to **Sapporo by March 31**, in exchange for **Moana's Tear**.
- Veratus leaves **Rootport** with Koric ostensibly to learn to protect his loved ones and uncover basilisk history. Koric secretly intends to deliver him to obtain Moana's Tear.
- The two travel in **Weston the Whale Taxi**; an **orca attack**, Koric's magic, and disaster at sea follow.
- **Eden's Isle**: they encounter ancient tortoise **Archaois** (spelling exactly as in the outline), essentially Koric's adoptive father; Koric has a strained relationship with him. Koric builds a slingshot/glider contraption to leave the island.
- **Grimroot Grove**: Veratus is separated and finds a giant-tree library and an old history of Dragex. He learns of the basilisk genocide around 17 years earlier.
- **Lady Wisdom**, an owl, speaks to Veratus. Talking with a bird is treated as rare and holy in Hokkaidon culture. She acts as though she knew Koric already; he is less reverent toward ancients.
- The pair are captured and taken to **Deviltown**, a cultish/tribal village in northeastern Grimroot Grove. The outline describes community bonds and magic that lowers inhibitions.
- Earlier chapter titles: **The Last of His Line**, **Moana's Revenge**, **The Ancients**, **Life's Alright in Deviltown**.
- All scene beats are historical draft content, not assumed final story.

## 2023 draft — alternate opening
Source: [Chapter 1: Rootport](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TKE0Pbhwj2udfgq8qJjJgaDF5mO8avubEH_Y3HAoW1s/edit)

- Veratus's sixteenth hatchday is a turning point. He works on his uncle/aunt's farm in Rootport and longs for a larger purpose; ships at the docks fascinate him.
- Koric appears as a fire salamander **wearing a cloak** and concealing himself because **amphibians face persecution**.
- Koric encourages Veratus to seek purpose and adventure. They depart together by sea.
- Fellow passengers become aggressive on discovering Koric is an amphibian; a **giant whale** attacks and swallows both protagonists.
- Different from the 2022 outline's method of travel and opening scenes.

Source: [2023 rough draft folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/16GTzjHh6r3PhRlCxGg9NCD-3lThYDywe)
- A 2023-04-30 message reports the rough draft had **16 chapters**. Visible chapter documents include Rootport, Weston, Grimroot Grove, Shimamaki, Trust, Foothills, Mount Yokai, Treasure, Glimmerton Springs, Pheonix [source title spelling], Destiny, Sapporo, and Guild of Kalos. These are **document titles only**, not proof that those events remain canon.

- [What is Dragex? (2020)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pCLR0ObuALa9B0IkcmG_WGk6V3isHAcv-0adGG2HY0o/edit): plot-driven story emphasizes Veratus's development and his relationship with mentor Koric; older route has Veratus meet Koric in Sapporo, contradictory to later Rootport meeting.
- [Chapter 2: Moana's Tear (2021)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/18Sqljx9XyV7gRrpBdClmnpnGizGHTLBH6s-C9HrmWV8/edit): a separate, much longer draft contains sharp banter about time, a whale taxi controlled by octopus conductor **Alfred**, and a pub called **Pacific Pub**; a green, red-eyed frog variously spelled **Psyte/Pyste** holds a glowing turquoise stone that attracts Koric's blue amulet. Spelling and whether the stone is Moana's Tear must be confirmed. Koric teaches Veratus that time is precious.

- A Feb 2024 writing account describes **Rootport**, a sea village of **1,000+ Feysians**, **Grandfather Tree**, vines, canopy, school, waters; Veratus's caretakers are chameleons **Uncle Roger and Aunt Claire** (Aunt Rose in 2022 outline).

## Death's Valley / Hokkaido / other older lore
- A self-emailed Dragex board-game concept dated **2022-01-07** describes **Moana's Oasis** becoming corrupted into **Death's Valley**, with water retained at its center, inside broader **Hokkaido** lore. This is compatible with but not automatically part of the current novel premise.
- A separate Hokkaido tale dated **2022-03-09** has magically created gnome guardians **Valia** (watches animals/forest of **Mount Yokai**) and **Talia** (watches northern humans from **Sapporo**, the capital of **Sylia**). A cursed river threatens the world's balance and Valia seeks Talia. Relationship to present-day Dragex continuity is uncertain.
- A Jan 2022 game design modeled Dragex as a Royal Game of Ur-inspired board game with reptile, mammal, amphibian, bird pieces and goal to crown 10 pieces; this is **game-history reference, not presumed novel canon**.

## Spelling conflicts / editorial checklist
1. **Aunt Rose vs Aunt Claire**: do not choose automatically.
2. **Archaois** in 2022 outline vs **Archeios** provisional name in newer Critz work: treat as unresolved identity/spelling.
3. **Psyte vs Pyste** in the same 2021 draft: unresolved.
4. **Feysians**, **Hokkaidon**, **Kuriku Port**, **Kaldro's Cape**, **Sylia**, **Shimamaki**: historical spellings, await confirmation.
5. **Quark**: no reliable evidence that Koric was called Quark; treat as a speech-to-text variant only if author confirms.
6. **Hokkaido**: appears as an important setting/region/world-name in old manuscripts, but its role in current Dragex must be confirmed.
7. 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024 outlines change who meets whom and where. Preserve versions, do not silently blend into one plot.

## Current author-confirmed canon (2026-10-11)
The first novel is **Moana's Tear**; **Koric** is a fire salamander, **Veratus** a basilisk lizard; the adventure ends with returning **Moana's Tear** to the **center of Death's Valley** and restoring rain and life. See `books/moanas-tear.md`.
