# Recovered characters and relationships

**Status:** Historical and version-specific, except the explicitly confirmed Koric/Veratus species and shared quest. Source IDs: [catalog](sources.md). No age, allegiance, personality list, or secondary identity is promoted to final canon merely by appearing here.

## The principal pair

### Koric

**Current confirmed:** fire salamander; travels with Veratus; returns Moana's Tear to the center of Death's Valley.

**N01 historical sheet:** amphibian; allegiance Birds; age unknown, at least 30; height 6 inches; origin Eden's Isle; friendly, clever, ambitious, adventurous, generous. **T14** has an older 7-inch version with black/red skin and different eye description, the fuller name **Koric Honono Ikimono**, and ownership of Pilgrim's Way after Archaíos leaves. **B01/N07** use black/red coloration and amber eyes; these physical specifics await current confirmation.

His recurring skills include archery, fire magic, practical invention, fast improvisation, and knowledge of the world. His temperament varies: a friendly mentor in one sheet, abrasive and withholding in scenes, morally compromised by secret motives in several outlines. These are actual version differences; do not reduce him to a universally kind mentor.

**C01:** he drank Fey blood during the Spring of Life, stole and hid the Tear, and was cursed not to truly die until its return. **H01:** Archaíos finds him apparently dead and sees him emerge from his funeral fire. **R10–R12:** former lives, the threatening **Koric the Exiled**, memory/rebirth, pirate/wartime/love experiences, and personal redemption. **X05:** a different death/abyss condition. No unified lifespan or reincarnation schedule is confirmed.

**A04/N02:** Archaíos is an adoptive-father figure whom Koric resents or refuses to credit. **N02:** Koric secretly wants to deliver Veratus to the guild to receive the Tear. **T02:** he breaks with the guild when Veratus's sacrifice becomes clear. **A05:** he uses Veratus to seek Enáretos while invoking restoration of balance. These cannot all be assumed to be one finalized motive.

### Veratus

**Current confirmed:** basilisk lizard; Koric's traveling companion in the Tear-return adventure.

**N01 historical sheet:** **brown basilisk**, Reptile allegiance, 15 years old, 8 inches, Rootport; resourceful, loyal, courageous, fierce, eager. **N02/N03/R01** instead make his sixteenth hatchday important. His exact current age and basilisk species below the general label remain unconfirmed.

**A01/B01/N02/N03/N05/N06:** raised by chameleons Roger and an aunt whose spelling/name varies. He knows he is different and longs for heritage, purpose, adventure, or the ability to protect home, depending on the version. **B01** makes him a detailed cartographer; **N03** gives him practiced parkour and palm-leaf gliding. **A04/R06** stage his discovery of running on water.

**N05:** Mr. and Mrs. **Parsley**, Uncle Roger and Aunt Claire, raised him; his work links waste collection, worm/maggot farming, and fruit-growing neighbors. The narrator says that they placed Veratus with Roger but is not identified by name in the passage. Do not automatically identify that narrator as Archaíos just because C02 uses him as a narrator elsewhere.

**T14:** a guild letter calls Veratus an offspring of **Axios** with **Keravna** blood. **T07/T13** propose a royal/revenge arc; **N02** calls him the last basilisk thought to survive in Hokkaido. These are historical lineage claims, not a completed family tree. **R11** connects his father's actions with Koric's burden. The current truth about parents, ancestor guilt, royal status, and surviving basilisks remains open.

## Supporting cast and version distinctions

**Moto:** sword/katana-bearing mouse, Koric's close ally, and in some outlines a father-figure member of the found family (T05, A03–A05, R03 onward). T11 says he moved from Kitami to Wakkanai at thirteen for training and later to Sapporo; these details need confirmation. His death appears in different plots: fighting the eastern ruler in T13 versus execution by Evander after a failed rescue in R16. Preserve the different circumstances.

**Roach:** kinkajou companion/cart driver in Alpha; helps rescue the panther cub, loves fruit/honey/food, and pulls the group's cart (A03–A05). He is not automatically present in the 2023 three-companion storyline.

**Archaíos / Archaois / Archaios:** tortoise, ancient witness, adoptive father, rescuer, and guide across several later sources. C02 proposes him as campaign narrator and rescuer of Veratus's egg. **T14 instead makes Archaíos a kappa**, founder of Pilgrim's Way. Do not treat the early kappa as biological confirmation of the tortoise or merge provisional newer “Archeios” without asking Phil.

**Psyte / Pyste:** source N01 says blacksmith tree frog; B02 describes a bright-green, red-eyed frog with mind-reading; T02 makes him an assassin; R04 gives him thrown/dart-like weapons; X01 gives a poison-themed crossover presentation. These details and spellings are not automatically interchangeable. B02's stone is explicitly the Tear.

**Díkaios:** B02 calls him the last peregrine falcon of the North and has him rescue Koric and Veratus from the whale wreck. This differs from the sheet's Southwest Wilderness origin; origin and later range might coexist, but no reconciliation is established.

**Enáretos:** named raven in N01, an empowering figure in T11/T13, and someone sought by Koric in A05. A raven also appears in R08/R10/R11, but the mere presence of a raven is not proof that every unnamed raven is Enáretos. The earlier assistant's **Korok the Raven Lord** assertion remains unverified.

**Sive:** guides Veratus out of the drowning whale in A02; clandestinely equips Koric in N07; possible betrayal in T05. All are versions of a bat concept, not confirmed chronological stages of one arc.

**Roger and Claire:** chameleon guardians. A01 spells the aunt **Clare**; B01 includes **Claire/Clair**; N02 calls her **Rose**; N05 explicitly uses **Mr. and Mrs. Parsley / Uncle Roger / Aunt Claire**. The May 2026 version is the latest recovered manuscript evidence, but Phil has not yet explicitly reconfirmed the final surname or aunt name in the current conversation.

**Lady Wisdom:** owl in N02, connected to an enormous library in a tree in Grimroot Grove. Kind toward Veratus, less warm toward Koric; speaks as if she knows him already. The outline says he has not previously met her. Do not invent why she knows him.

**Weston / Bailey:** whale transport systems with inhabited interiors. A01–A02 use **Bailey**, a female blue whale; later sources use **Weston**, usually male. Renaming or separate whales is unresolved. **Alfred** is an octopus conductor in those later whale scenes. T14 also uses Alfred for a frog in a political conversation; do not silently merge the two.

**Karuk:** C03 labels an inside-a-giant-crocodile map **Karuk (Doshan)**. “Doshan” also accompanies Weston in that deck. The taxonomy/term is not explained in the readable text.

**Barnaby of Quillcrest:** horned lizard encountered around Glimmerton Springs in R09; reads minds and heals Koric. No approved connection to other healers is established.

**Evander:** silver fox and Kalos Guild leader in R14–R16; antagonist of the heist and responsible for Moto's death in that draft.

**Drago:** antagonist in T02's guild story. **Draco** is a separately spelled lizard in N01/T11. Do not merge them from sound alone.

**Mogul:** eastern/Kitami antagonist in T07/T14. **Akuno** appears in T11's eastern-tyrant fragments. T13 proposes an unnamed ruler's sister inheriting power. Their identities and relationships are not settled as one unified antagonist family.

**Daku:** a voice advising Koric in his office in T14. Species and identity are not supplied in the relevant scene. **Axios** and **Keravna** are lineage names in the same draft; no species or complete genealogy should be invented.

## Smaller everyday-world characters

- **Martha Carrington:** beaver rehabilitator with a family at Carrington Grounds; her dam regulates the Sapporo canal (A04). She conflicts with Koric about protecting vulnerable wildlife.
- **Pluto:** rescued panther cub, named by Veratus (A03–A04). The draft starts by identifying the cub as female but later slips pronouns; do not canonize the slip.
- **Billy the bilby:** rehabilitated young animal at Martha's house (A04), distinct from all Billy-named rats/merchants.
- **Ame:** young salamander serving at Pilgrim's Way (A05, T14). **Yuki** is mentioned as an expected coworker in T14, without a supplied species.
- **Keeko:** frog companion in T14/T16/X02. Do not silently rename him Psyte because both are frogs.
- **Billy Bert** and **Billy Bob:** rat father/son associated with Billy Bugs Pet Shop; **Willy**, **Philly**, and another robber appear in T14. H03 separately confirms the shop as rat-family-owned on Tulip Street.
- **James:** skink bartender in N05.
- **Bill:** chipmunk merchant in N03; distinct from the rat shop family.
- **Gary:** gecko; **Bart:** anole; **Eugene:** mentioned in festival/catch dialogue with species unprovided (N03).
- **Kassy:** participant in an early Sapporo tax debate, with insufficient species evidence in the read scene (T14).
- **Ayla:** only a fragment saying she is lost (X03); no species, role, or age established.
- **Sinbad:** name invoked concerning older sea conditions in A02; no biography recovered.
- **Hok** and **Franklin:** dwarf-related names in the mixed older T11 fragments; not confirmed inhabitants of current novel continuity.

## Separate adaptation and crossover cast

**N04 — children's adaptation:** **Annie** and **Lucy**, young foxes of Whisperwood; **Baer**, a worm prince; **Sylvie**, silver-coated wolf guardian; Baer's unnamed father is called king. A forest fire brings them together. This is a separate draft, not proof that every animal category has the same civil status as in N05.

**X01 — PowPass crossover:** **Moto, Pyste/Psyte, Koric, Veratus, Tony, Tanoshi, Dragex, Moana**. Dragex is labeled dragon and Moana sea serpent; elemental icons are crossover design evidence, not automatic novel powers. Tony/Tanoshi are not assigned ages or biographies here.

**C06/D04 — game units:** a generic Wood Mouse is not automatically Moto; generic lizards/basilisks are not automatically Veratus. Species units, cosmetic skins, unlock tiers, and named Conquerors belong in their own game documents until a cross-media identity is explicit.
