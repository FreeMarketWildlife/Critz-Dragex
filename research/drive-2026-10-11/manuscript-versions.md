## Earliest Sapporo-centered draft — T14, T16, X02

T14 begins with a now-alternate history: Fey/fairies and Akuma Yōkai fight, blood transforms animals, dwarves transmit literature and a common language, and Sapporo becomes a city. Archaíos is a **kappa**, founds a small-creature pub, and later hands it to Koric.

In 655 AC, Koric argues for tax-supported defense against Mogul's expansion. The scene gives him political contradictions, civic concerns, a pub business, and a private counselor called **Daku**. Veratus seeks magical apprenticeship but is dismissed. A guild letter naming **Axios**, **Keravna**, and **Koric Honono Ikimono** changes Koric's response. A comic violent robbery at Billy Bugs Pet Shop introduces **Keeko**, and Koric later pays for Veratus to stay at Scales and Tales.

T16 and X02 overlap substantially: Veratus wakes at the tavern, eats melonpan, and looks for board 3B during the weekly job-board replacement. These are distinct files but not two independent adventures. The existence of a 2019 source moves the recovered document trail earlier than the first-pass report suggested.

## Early political plot — T11, T13, T07, T05

T11 is a collection of exploratory fragments rather than a coherent finished chapter. It includes a freedom quest against an eastern tyrant, Hongo/chisana/yahse terminology, northern/southern travel, a dwarf song, earlier humans, Koric founding Eastlake, and a seven-member party receiving blue-fire gifts from a raven during a Blood Moon. It contains unfinished sentences and alternate openings.

T13's proposed sequence: Enáretos gives the party magic; Sapporo argues; Koric kills the eastern leader while Moto dies; the leader's sister escalates war; Koric is exiled to Rishiri; Veratus faces hanging; Koric returns riding Enáretos and makes Veratus ruler. The listed arcs are Koric gaining wisdom, Veratus maturity, Moto humility.

T07 has a related but distinct revenge plot involving Veratus's parents and **Mogul**, Moto's death, a dark cloud after the enemy's death, and an army approaching Sapporo. Its third act is not fully supplied. T05 records found-family and possible betrayal/defection ideas, including Sive, Azule, and Draco. These versions must not be treated as the finalized ending of *Moana's Tear*.

## Alpha chapters — A01–A05

1. **Kaldro's Cape:** Veratus leaves his chameleon guardians with a dagger and a desire to learn his origins. His sea journey brings him to **Bailey**, a whale with inhabited interiors, and **Alfred**, an octopus conductor. A fare in **sal** and small-animal body scale are specified in this version.
2. **Underwater:** **Sive** helps Veratus survive a whale disaster/sea danger. A tortoise encounter and a request to plant a seed in Rootport follow. The version contains older sea-history hints and the name **Sinbad**.
3. **Pluto:** Veratus meets **Koric, Moto, and Roach** while a panther cub is threatened by hunters. The basilisk mountain kingdom and a long reign are discussed. The group dynamic is not identical to the later two-protagonist opening.
4. **The First Sign of a King:** Veratus names the panther cub **Pluto**; **Martha Carrington** and her beaver family care for wildlife. Koric and Martha disagree about this intervention. Her dam relates to the canal toward Sapporo. Veratus's water-running ability develops, and Koric discusses childhood/Archaíos.
5. **The Great City:** Sapporo, **Pilgrim's Way**, and **Ame** appear. Koric's relationship to the pub and hidden plans for Veratus become clearer. Enáretos and a **three-month** Blood Moon deadline are part of this version.

Do not correct every rough-draft pronoun, animal identity, or meeting place by silently borrowing from another draft.

## Beta chapters — B01–B02

**Exhausted in Paradise:** Veratus has mapped the southern forest and grows frustrated with a safe, comfortable farm life. He disputes his guardians' view that obstacles simply slow life down. His attempt to sell the map at Rootport's docks draws Koric's interest. Koric leaves by boat as Veratus contemplates home, and Veratus jumps after him.

**Moana's Tear:** the pair banter about the value of time. Koric takes Veratus into the **Weston Taxi**, where Alfred conducts passengers and **Pacific Pub** hosts Psyte/Pyste. The frog possesses the glowing stone; Koric's amulet responds to it. Sea-creature attacks turn the inhabited whale into a wreck. The full chapter explicitly identifies the stone as the Tear and states Koric's goal to restore Death's Valley. Psyte hands it over. **Díkaios**, the peregrine falcon, rescues Koric and Veratus.

This is not the same acquisition as N02's guild payment or R14's Sapporo heist. The discarded T04 dialogue includes an abandoned water-trigger joke; its presence is not permission to restore it to the current manuscript.

## 2021 planning and Charlie prologue — T01/T02/T03/N07

T01 places the Blood Moon about **thirteen days** away, involves a disturbing aunt's dream, a whale crisis, Archaíos, a sword, and an improvised flight. T02 makes Koric's guild association morally central: Veratus leaves to protect home, an amulet is stolen, the guild intends a sacrifice with a death blade, and Koric eventually rejects that goal. **Drago** appears in the antagonist material.

T03 explores what the Tear does, what contact with water means, why the valley is dying, and how the climax might work. Because it includes the author's own unresolved questions, proposed solutions must remain proposals.

N07 opens in a storm at Kuriku Port. Koric covertly asks **Sive** for his bow and equipment. The guild's secret **Class V** job is to bring a surviving basilisk from Cloud Forest to Sapporo unharmed by **March 31**; author comments say Koric reaches Rootport on March 16. Another comment links the storm/sea danger to a sandbar destroyed by the guild and the release of creatures from **Shinkai Bay**.

## 2022 outline — N02

The prologue explicitly bargains for **Moana's Tear in exchange for delivering Veratus** to the Kalos Guild. Veratus lives in Rootport, is thought to be the last basilisk, turns sixteen on **March 16, 17 ADF**, and was adopted as an egg by Roger and **Rose** in this version.

Koric rescues the family, and Veratus leaves to protect loved ones and learn basilisk history. On **Weston**, they play the **Dragex board game** and discuss Moana's Revenge before an orca attack. Koric uses magic to escape. They wash up on **Eden's Isle**, meet **Archaois**, and learn the strained adoptive-father history. Koric builds an improvised slingshot/glider, and the companions separate over Grimroot Grove.

Veratus finds a giant-tree library and **the Book of Dragex**, learns about the basilisk genocide, and meets **Lady Wisdom**, an owl. Koric is less reverent toward her; she acts as though she knows him even though he has not met her. The pair are captured and taken to **Deviltown**, a cultish/tribal community using inhibition-lowering magic. The surviving outline stops before a fully developed book resolution.

## Alternate 2022 opening — N03

Veratus's sixteenth hatchday arrives near the spring festival. His chameleon family farms mealworms. Roger recognizes that he is preparing to leave and gives affectionate support. Veratus intends to cross Cloud Forest to Kuriku Port and sail to **Kuroso City** after the festival.

At the docks, **Bill**, a chipmunk merchant, invites him for a drink/story, but Bill's ship is stolen. Veratus uses a cut palm leaflet to glide onto it. He investigates below deck and is knocked unconscious by an amber-eyed figure. The text does not explicitly name that attacker in the ending passage; do not fill the identity from appearance alone.

## Later openings — N06 and N05

**N06, modified February 2024:** Veratus is a thoughtful young basilisk in a Rootport of more than a thousand Feysians. School and his sense of belonging are explored. Roger and Claire raise him, Grandfather Tree stands, and a market disturbance draws him into action. It conflicts with B01's repurposed/fallen Grandfather Tree and does not resolve the full book plot.

**N05, May 2026:** narration explains named critters and civilizations, the Sustainer **Allohim**, free will and purpose, and the **Parsley** family. Rootport's communal economic relationships are detailed. The unidentified narrator claims to have placed Veratus with Roger. The excerpt then turns to skink bartender **James**, **Dragon's Ale**, and a pub game. This is the latest recovered opening, not proof that every earlier chapter has been revised to match it.

## Other fragments and adaptation

**N04:** Whisperwood, foxes Annie/Lucy, worm prince Baer, wolf guardian Sylvie, and a forest fire. Treat as separate adaptation material.

**X03:** possible underground-world direction, Veratus finding a black feather that summons a dark creature, the idea that swords/sword arts are new, Deviltown, and “Ayla is lost.” These are fragments, not a completed alternate manuscript. **X04** gives an opening line denying prior humans in Hokkaido. **X05** suggests titles “The Legend of Koric” and “The Legend of Veratus” and a different curse condition.

## Practical continuity rule

When drafting a scene, first identify its chosen version and approved current facts. Do not combine the Sapporo apprenticeship, Rootport map sale, birthday rescue, pirate-ship attack, whale heist, and guild bargain as if the character first met Koric six different times in the same book.
