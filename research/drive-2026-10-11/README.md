## Coverage and limits

The main `/Google Drive/Literature/Dragex` folder and all its manuscript, outline, short-story, tabletop, and Critz design-document branches were traversed. Broader Drive searches for **Dragex, Critz, Hokkaido, Moana, Koric**, and name variants including **Crtiz** recovered material outside the main folder. Additional current game names came from the retrieved October 9 conversation, not from nonexistent Drive design documents.

These are organized extracts and summaries linked back to originals, **not verbatim copies of every source**. The original Drive files were not modified. No claim is made that every possible file in the entire account was discovered: unindexed documents, inaccessible files, image-only content, archives, and assets can contain more information.

Binary builds, model/texture transfers, purchased plug-ins, sound-effect assets, and accounting/receipts were not exhaustively inspected for lore. Relevant mixed personal text files were read only to recover the lore fragments; unrelated personal content is not reproduced here. Illustrations and slide visuals were not comprehensively audited; source diagram connections and details absent from readable text were not guessed. The supplied conversation maps are transcribed in the Hokkaido file, but their image bytes are not archived here.

The source files returned complete text ranges where readable, but extracted text is not a guarantee that every embedded image or tabular relationship was preserved. Read the original for exact prose, diagrams, formatting, or line-by-line manuscript work.
