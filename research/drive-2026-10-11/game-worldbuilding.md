# Game worldbuilding and cross-media records

**Status:** Recovered design evidence, not automatic novel canon. Sources: [catalog](sources.md). Current titles and unassigned eras: [game registry](../../games/registry.md).

## 1. Original Critz shooter — C04 and later design files

C04 describes the original third-person shooter, inspired by classic Battlefront-style combat. Its early class concepts include a gripping/climbing gecko marine, a weasel sniper, a defensive turtle heavy, a scavenging raccoon engineer, a gliding flying-squirrel assassin, and a rolling armadillo tank. Later critter and unlock files move toward broader species access and equipment choice. These are design versions, not six immutable social castes.

The setting uses Hokkaido locations familiar from Dragex. Shared place names establish deliberate world reuse, but do not date every multiplayer match or make every repeatable death/resurrection literal story history.

## 2. The older campaign has historical chapters — C02, October 2025

**Narrator:** Archaíos the tortoise.

**Mission 1 — Battle of Mount Yokai:** Fey against Fey, without ordinary playable animals or guns; medieval-style staffs, wands, bows and swords; a tree-related engine/objective and seed retrieval. The draft also places Dragex attacking Moana within this early sequence. A cutscene moves from blood-bearing snowmelt and transformed animals to Veratus's father ruling the mountain.

**Mission 2 — Fall of the Basilisks:** the player fights as the mammal side using military weapons against basilisks using nonmagical swords, bows, and warhammers. Archaíos rescues an egg and carries it to Rootport.

**Mission 3 — Throne Room:** heading/idea, not a fully written mission in the recovered text.

This campaign concept spans earlier events, but **no final current game age is assigned**. C01 separates Dragex's attack on Moana in Age 1 from the Fey war in Age 2, so the old mission's combined sequence is a conflict to resolve. Modern weapons in a proposed scene do not by themselves authorize us to declare its age.

The October 2026 **Critz Campaign** concept is an open-world standalone game. Whether C02's missions become its main story, prologue, flashbacks, another game's campaign, or unused material is still Phil's decision.

## 4. Modes, lobbies, and minigames — C05/C09

**Conquest:** teams manage reinforcements and capture positions. **Duel:** short competitive one-on-one structure. **Blitz:** faster/unrestricted variant. **Apex:** predator/juggernaut-style idea. **Hide & Hunt:** hiding/hunting mode. The **Eastlake** lobby includes social/minigame ideas such as Critter Says, pinball, darts, pool, poker, rat-slap, whack-a-mole, parkour, fishing, farming, and surfing.

A game mode is not another standalone title. “Duel Arena” references in the older progression/achievement context include high-stakes item exchange; they are not proof that the newer **Critz Duel Arena** platform fighter has those same rules.

C09 uses many real-world/media quotations and references as achievement names. Those jokes do not establish the named reference characters, companies, humans, or fictional franchises as residents of Hokkaido. Likewise, account verification mentioning a human is interface language, not a contradiction of the novel setting's no-human rule.

## 5. Playable creatures and anatomy — C06/C07/D03/D04

Recovered species/unit ideas include lizard, frog, turtle, salamander, mouse, rabbit, weasel, flying squirrel, armadillo, bat, Diplocaulus, gopher, pangolin, mouse lemur, possum, and Surinam toad. Broader progression references include reptile, amphibian, mammal, avian, and invertebrate categories. Generic animal units are distinct from named novel characters and from the newer MOBA's named **Conquerors**.

D04's generic **Wood Mouse** is described as 10 cm and 20 grams with Hasty and baseline game stats. It is not identified as Moto. D03's passives include **Hasty, Shell, Hopper, Hydrophobic, Sticky, Glide, Spiky, Borrower** (source spelling for tunneling), **Flight, Slither, Health Regeneration, Roller, Swinger**. These connect gameplay to movement/anatomy; their numbers and balance effects are not universal story physics.

D02 is a model/material/rigging/animation production pipeline. D01 is largely visual and could not be fully inspected via the attempted page renderer. This audit does not certify final visual appearances from text alone.

C07 uses common/uncommon/rare/extinct/mythical-style progression and weapon categories such as **Military, Magic, Primal, Tamer**. Flattened extraction does not reliably preserve every diagram edge; exact unlock requirements should be checked in the original before implementation. Rarity is a game progression label, not proof that a named hero is a separate biological species.

## 6. Equipment and the technology question — C08/C11/C12

Four loadout slots are **Primary, Secondary, Melee, Utility**. Recovered equipment spans several visual/technological styles:

- Primary: assault rifle, shotgun, sniper rifle, rocket launcher, bow, crossbow, wizard staff.
- Secondary: blaster pistol, slingshot, splash blaster, wand, flamethrower.
- Melee: fists/grapple, knife, katana/deflection, bo staff/wind-like push, shield/bash, tomahawk/throw, warhammer.
- Utility/nature/magic: grenades, smoke, deployed resources, mines, glider, bottles of gas/fire/lightning/ice/air, dandelion for slowed falling, maple seed as a spinning projectile, and exploratory invisibility-cloak/void-dagger ideas.

These are conceptual **fictional game designs**. Damage values, effects and cooldowns belong in gameplay data, not a lore chronology. The availability of a rifle in a multiplayer build does not establish who invented it, how arcane technology works, whether humans introduced it, or which age every match occupies. Phil will place the games.

C11 is a checklist, not proof that all models, animations, audio, multiplayer features or balance steps are implemented. C12 lists skins, accessories, emotes, finishers, boosts, starter equipment and bells. Cosmetic variants are not automatically shapeshifting powers or a claim that every skin has a separate biography. C10 prioritizes fun and encouraging challenge over frustrating progression; that is design intent, not in-universe doctrine.

## 7. Dragex tabletop game — G01 and N02

A separate January 2022 rulebook survives. The goal is to **crown ten pieces**. It uses prey, predators and apex pieces; movement, attack and income stats; dice-based movement/conflict; ready/unready states; and **bells** as currency. Tile types include Home, Forest, Blessing, Event, Plains, Death, Crown, Mountain and Water. Rangers can occupy mountains; boats enable water movement.

Forest/Blessing/Event effects include advancement, ambush, maps, camps, traps, ranger actions, magical archetypes and **Dragex**. **Moana's Revenge / Anger / Rage** names appear in different effect lists. A **Death Die** introduces immediate effects. These names draw on the fictional mythology but are **board-game rules**, not direct descriptions of what cosmic beings can do in the novel.

The draft has internal rules inconsistencies, such as different fight rewards in separate passages and an empty How To Win section despite the stated crowning objective. Do not treat it as a polished final ruleset. Its existence as a physical project and N02's scene of the protagonists playing Dragex are separate evidence. Its place in **Critz Party**, if any, is unconfirmed.

## 8. PowPass–Dragex crossover — X01, July–August 2025

The document lays out a reward progression with character boards, hoodies, eggs, character cards, boxes, PowBucks and season-related reward names. It includes **Tanoshi, Moto, Psyte/Pyste, Koric, Veratus, Tony, Dragex, Moana**.

The character/type area associates **Moto with death**, **Psyte/Pyste with poison**, **Koric with fire**, **Veratus with ice**, **Tony with skateboarding**, **Tanoshi with nature**, **Dragex with mountain/dragon imagery**, and **Moana with water/sea-serpent imagery**. The prose explicitly labels Dragex **Dragon** and Moana **Sea Serpent** in that crossover table. The document is partly tabular/visual; do not infer unshown descriptions or biography from blank cells.

These are useful historical art/product directions, but not automatic canonical powers or a seven-age placement. An “egg” reward does not prove that its named character literally hatches from that item in the Dragex books. Pow Vista and this collaboration have not been placed into the shared timeline by this audit.
