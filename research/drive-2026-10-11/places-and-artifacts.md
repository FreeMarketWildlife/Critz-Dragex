## Rootport details by version

**B01:** Veratus maps trees, rocks, holes, hills, homes, animal territories, and natural resources. He and his guardians farm coffee, tea, and cocoa. He tries to sell his map to merchants. **Grandfather Tree** has become the **Woodland Inn**, and Roger and Claire recall being betrothed there.

**N06:** Grandfather Tree is a standing tree Veratus climbs, Rootport has over a thousand **Feysians**, and school, adolescent belonging, trade, and a market commotion form the opening.

**N03:** the narrator contrasts a later, road-connected Rootport with the earlier port receiving sea traders on Wednesdays. Veratus's daily route includes **Bear Creek**, a rope swing, a farm clearing, a pink ipê/tabebuia tree, **Boris Bridge**, and the docks. A seasonal festival and meals from insect farming are important. A palm leaflet becomes an improvised glider when a ship is stolen.

**N05:** Rootport is east of **Kalos' Cape**, hemmed in by foggy Cloud Forest. The Parsley family farms worms/maggots and exchanges waste-derived soil with fruit farms. **James**, a skink bartender, serves **Dragon's Ale**; a game of pong is introduced. The exact pub's identity is not supplied, so do not call it Pilgrim's Way merely because that name appears elsewhere.

These details are useful scene material, but combine them only after checking what Phil wants retained.

## Sapporo and its institutions

**Pilgrim's Way:** a pub/inn and recurring meeting place (A05, B02, N02, R13, T14). In T14 it is founded by Archaíos, a kappa, then transferred to Koric; in A05 Koric speaks of building/owning it. Staff include **Ame**, while **Yuki** is mentioned in T14. Menu names include Grassroot Stout, Dragon Ale, and Johnny Fries in A05. The same brand name in different drafts does not establish identical buildings, ownership history, or menus.

**Guild of Kalos / Kalos Guild:** powerful organization commissioning jobs and pursuing Veratus in different versions. T14 calls it a cult and a source of magical apprenticeship directions. N02 makes its payment to Koric the Tear in exchange for Veratus. T02 plans a sacrifice using a death blade. R14 depicts its dungeons, stored Tear fragments, and silver-fox leader **Evander**. N07's job is secret/Class V, excludes outside help, and has a March 31 deadline.

**Scales and Tales Tavern:** reptile-friendly lodging across a range of sizes (T14). **Billy Bugs Pet Shop:** rat-family business on Tulip Street, with T14 additionally placing it at Birch Road. **South/West/East/North Streets:** early Sapporo civic layout in T14, organized partly around body sizes and trades. **Bulletin board 3B:** work meeting point; the boards are refreshed on Fridays in T16/X02. **Hummingbird Drive:** an alternate pub location in T11.

**Carrington Grounds:** Martha Carrington's beaver-family wildlife rehabilitation home and dam, with a canal connection toward Sapporo (A04). This location dramatizes disagreement about intervening in wild animals' lives.

## Inside living creatures

**Bailey / Weston:** whale transport with corridors, rooms, service staff, and businesses; differing names, sex descriptions, and routes across A01–A02, B02, and R02. **Alfred** is an octopus conductor. B02 contains **Pacific Pub** inside Weston. R02 describes a more developed internal city with restaurants and a theater. The user has not yet approved an exact anatomy or a shared chronology explaining every version.

**Karuk (Doshan):** giant-crocodile interior battlefield in C03, with stomach hazards. **Weston (Doshan)** also appears in that map deck. “Doshan” is preserved without a fabricated definition. **Sakana** is a river monster in a brief T11 fragment about the mother of Koric's children; no complete story or species is supplied.

## Terms worth preserving without inventing definitions

**Fey, Feysian/Feysians, critter, arcane, Spring of Life, phoenix curse, ADF, AC, Hongo, chisana, yahse, Tori, Ethería, Doshan.** Some have descriptions above; others are merely mentioned in a source. In T11 “laws of Tori” and “Kingdoms of Ethería” are invoked but not comprehensively defined. Do not promote Ethería to the name of the planet or translate these into a finished taxonomy without Phil.
