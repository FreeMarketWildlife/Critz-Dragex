Coastline source & design boundaries

Alaska and New Zealand reference coastlines: Natural Earth 1:50m, public domain. Main-island Hokkaido outline: JapanPrefGeoJson, derived from Data of Japan, public domain. Simplified and mapped using a Lambert azimuthal equal-area transform, then normalized to the specified spherical polygon area. Terrain colors are procedural illustration, not surveyed elevation. Coordinates, pins and new celestial bodies are design proposals. Alaska and New Zealand use Natural Earth reference coastlines, rotated and rescaled to author-requested sizes; their exact rotations and positions are design choices. Polar textures depict predominantly sea ice, not new polar continents. Remaining ocean-colored surface is not a fully confirmed map. Timeline colors, lake shape, river paths and drought contours are visual design drafts; the seven ages and supplied geography anchors follow the author’s account.
