THE MILLENNIUM PASSAGE

A designed encounter with a sparse outer debris ring every 1,000 years. The bright inner rings remain safely inside Libriam’s orbit.

How these orbits were chosen

Kepler orbits with masses, eccentricity and nested parents. Hill and Roche checks screen the spacing; long-term tidal stability has not been simulated.

The millennium debris episode is an authored schedule, not a prediction from gravity. Continuous rings would be crossed twice per tilted orbit.

Kronos: 0.72 AU. Zuhal: 2.40 AU. The K dwarf is a proposed 0.75 solar masses and 0.30 solar luminosity. Libriam’s atmosphere and oceans remain part of the fantasy world.
