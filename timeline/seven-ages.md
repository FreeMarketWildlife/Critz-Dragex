# Seven Ages of Libriam — master timeline

**Status:** Canonical events and transitions confirmed directly by author on **October 11, 2026**. The **Seventh Age remains provisional**. Recovered earlier outline: [April 2026 — [Critz] Lore](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16ctzSVLICRg27OQlmpuGRzuF0lv8yu37yvBHDzkSCnw/edit?usp=drivesdk).

## Boundary notes and precise semantics

- **Later October 12 animation account:** Dragex attacks Moana at the lake, then burrows beneath Mount Yokai. His **complete sleep**, not the start of digging, still ends Age 1. Moana's remains should be predominantly **bones by roughly halfway through Age 2**, refining the previously open decay timing; no equal design-unit spans are converted to years. The immense ribs remain visible in Ages 3 and 4. Clip timing and attack choreography are proposals.

- **October 12, 2026 author clarification:** the central island in Lake Moana is **Moana’s Tear**, formed over Moana’s skull with part exposed. Her body slowly decays there; immense ribs are visible around Death’s Valley after Age 2 and during **Ages 3 and 4**. This establishes visible remains in those eras, not a new death date or transition. The lake's restoration at the Age-3/4 boundary does not remove the author-confirmed Age-4 ribs. The named island's relationship to the portable Tear artifact remains unspecified.

- Age 1 ends **at the moment** Dragex is completely asleep, not simply when Moana dies or when Fey arrive.
- Age 2 ends **at the death of the last Fey fighting at Mount Yokai**, not the global extinction of the species. The battle virtually wipes out the participating Fey and destroys their dominant civilization. **Some nonparticipants, including young/newborn Fey, and Fey on the other two continents survive**; even complete death among battle participants is described with some intentional hyperbole. Age 3 begins at this transition.
- The Fey-blooded animals arise from the blood-stained water **after** the Fey's final battle. The first generation includes **Archaíos**, who personally drinks the water. The precise date of Koric's curse and other events within Age 3 awaits further author explanation.
- Age 3 ends **with restoration of the Tear**; that allows Koric eventually to die, but the author did not specify whether he dies at the transition.
- Age 4 begins with the **reign of Veratus** over the reborn Kingdom of Moana; do not assume a specific coronation ceremony or dates.
- The Age-4 discovery of **two other continents** does not mean that safe, verified settlement or round-trip voyages were achieved. No traveling critters return, while birds travel between lands and some great aquatic creatures have seen them.
- **Arcane wars** (nuclear-like in impact) mark the end of Age 4. **Allohim's cleansing and judgement** mark the end of Age 5, not the title of a separate Sixth Age.
- The transition into Age 6 is tightly related to the cleansing, ocean submersion/reemergence, garden, and first humans, but the interval between those acts has not been quantified.
- The defining distinction is now author-confirmed: **Age 6 has relative peace but repeated failure and conflict; Age 7 is sinless, deathless and everlasting**. The precise Sixth/Seventh transition is **the defeat of the last enemy on Libriam**, aided by Allohim, after humanity has filled and subdued all three great continents.
- Allohim's **Kingdom descends to Libriam** upon final victory; all its creatures submit to him and do his will. Mankind rules there, secure from any renewed sin by Allohim's explicit promise.
- **Age-7 cosmic exploration** reaches other created planets, where sin remains a problem and Libriam's explorers help inhabitants know and obey Allohim. **Earth remains permanently beyond contact**, as earlier established; the mission must not be rewritten as visiting Earth.
- The Age-6 enemies include Fallout-mutated creatures and **ancient Fey**. Their presence no longer contradicts Age 2, because Fey survive the Mount Yokai catastrophe on Libriam. Their exact identities, generations, causes, and allegiances are not established.

## The book's two periods

**Event timeline (Age 3):** Koric and Veratus travel to return the Tear; the return ends the age.  
**Composition/narration timeline (Age 4):** Archaíos, very old and approaching death on Eden's Isle, writes *Moana's Tear* for his 19-year-old descendant Olivia (“Olive”), who studies history at the Great Library and school inside the Grandfather Tree of Grimroot Grove. Annual migrating birds carry his letters. He believes he has only one opportunity left to send them.

See [book narrative frame](../books/moanas-tear-frame.md). Do not assume Olive participates in the original adventure simply because she receives the narrative.

## Superseded dates and unresolved calendars

- The first October 11 statement that *Moana's Tear* **begins in Age 4** was revised by the author later that evening; the canonical **events belong in Age 3** while its **writing happens in Age 4**. Preserve this distinction in summaries, reading guides, and future game placements.
- The April 2026 outline called Age 6 **Judgement**. Author now explicitly assigns judgement to the **end of Age 5** and renames the Sixth Age **Man**.
- Older calendar labels (**539–655 AC**, **17 ADF**, and **144 years after Tear removal**) are not converted to age numbers until further author decisions.
- The historical April source uses “Redemption” once as an Age-3 heading but its overview says **Redemptive Opportunity**; latest author explicitly chooses **Redemptive Opportunity**.
- The April 2026 sketch of a peaceful Seventh Age is now superseded by the definitive later author account: the Age of Peace **begins with the last enemy's defeat**, is **everlasting**, and includes **space exploration and aid to other worlds**.
- The earlier phrase “humans arrive from the western ocean around Age 6” has been replaced as the first-origin model by the direct creation/placement of a man and woman by Allohim on Eden's Isle at the start of Age 6. Any residual western travel stories await clarification.

## The Battle of Mount Yokai is not a species-wide extinction

**Latest clarification (October 12, 2026):** “every Fey died” means the Fey civilization was destroyed by an exceptionally deadly battle. The author estimates **about 99.99%** of Fey participated as an evocative approximation, **not an exact population count**. Many, perhaps almost all, participants died; even the absolute wording about every combatant is acknowledged as rhetorical. **Fey who abstained**, especially **young Fey**, and **Fey living on the other two great continents of Libriam**, remained alive. The end-of-Age-2 boundary is the **death of the last Fey fighting at Mount Yokai**, not the death of the last Fey globally.

## Basilisk kingdom and Koric's last two lives — October 14, 2026

Author interview clarification: **during Age 3**, Veratus's father rules Mount Yokai and employs Koric's immediately previous incarnation as counselor. The king bans magic while demanding its private use, then executes that incarnation with the Ice Blade. This execution precipitates a united assault led by mammals, with the hosts of heaven also coming; the basilisk kingdom is destroyed, Mount Yokai is burned, and the earth shakes. No basilisks or eggs remain on the mountain in Phil's account; worldwide extinction is not established, and Veratus's means of survival remains open. **This is distinct from the Age-2 Fey battle.**

In the current incarnation, Koric seeks Veratus for delivery to the Guild of Kalos, which possesses the Tear or part of it. During the journey, the Cave of Origins reunites his soul fragments and memories; recalling his execution and seeing the father's likeness in Veratus, he tries to kill him until a raven intervenes. Cave-before-mountain versus cave-after-mountain remains undecided.

The raven knows Veratus is destined to rule the restored Moana's Tear location/kingdom and that **Koric's final death will occur in Death's Valley**, allowing his spirit to go to Allohim. **The Tear's return still ends Age 3**; the precise death timing relative to that transition and its manner remain unspecified. See [the current book account](../books/moanas-tear.md).

## Opening journey order — October 14, 2026

Phil settles the relative sequence within Age 3: **Prairie Bridge inn revelation → dream-driven detour to Cave of Origins near East Lake → soul reunion and fight → raven intervention/prophecy → campfire confession that night → Mount Yokai and king's chamber**. ## Egg rescue timing — October 14, 2026

After executing Koric's previous incarnation, the basilisk king sees the united Hokkaido army continuing its advance **despite the thunderclouds**. Before the kingdom's destruction, he begs the widely renowned Archaíos to save his **last remaining egg**, fearing the end of his line and species. This is Veratus; the king does not yet know the hatchling's sex. The rescue request is thus after the execution and during the approaching assault; precise escape timing remains unspecified.

Koric's previous incarnation **frees all remaining prisoners** after the king starts imprisoning people for trivial offenses to supply Ice Blade executions. With the mammalian-kingdom-led united armies already approaching, the king finds no prisoners left and **executes the counselor in their place**. Preferred author direction: lightning still strikes, but the armies continue. Special Ice Blade/phoenix-curse interaction remains an alternative under consideration. **Axios is confirmed as Veratus's father and the king**. ## Sapporo handover and escape — October 15, 2026

Before Sapporo, Koric teaches Veratus Fey magic. In the **artifact-filled cult museum beneath the keep in the king's ward**, Veratus discovers he is being traded to Kalos for Moana's Tear. He enters custody willingly because he trusts **Allohim's promise through Drakos** of ruling Lake Moana, without knowing the rescue plan. Koric obtains the Tear, Veratus takes the Ice Blade, and Moto reveals his undercover guild disguise; they fight their way out. **Veratus's first killing triggers lightning through the ceiling**, paying off the blade/lightning images seen earlier in Mount Yokai's king's chamber and halls. Exact dates and rescue choreography remain open. See [book notes](../books/moanas-tear.md).

## First Ice Blade killing and Sapporo collapse — October 15, 2026

At Sapporo's artifact museum, Veratus is **horrified by his first killing**, but has no time to process it: lightning strikes through the ceiling and rubble buries **Koric, Veratus and Moto** in total darkness. They must escape the enclosed space, then continue carrying Moana's Tear toward Death's Valley. Exact escape, injuries and next route remain open. See [book notes](../books/moanas-tear.md).

## Flight through Sapporo after the museum collapse — October 15, 2026

Koric, Veratus and Moto are buried in pitch-black underground rubble, like survivors of a cave-in. They locate one another, confirm the Tear is with them, and work their way out. Sapporo is now on high alert. The city is massive despite its tiny-critter population, making escape difficult; **amphibians are unwelcome, especially in the royal inner district**, putting Koric at added risk. The single killing/lightning strike damages the museum area, not necessarily the whole city. See [book notes](../books/moanas-tear.md).

## Western Kingdom and flight from Sapporo — October 15, 2026

Mammals hold political power; **Sapporo is the capital of the Western Kingdom**. Its king is a relatively small mammal; Phil leans toward a **platypus**, not confirmed. After emerging from the underground collapse, Koric, Veratus and Moto run, pursued by Sapporo's guards, then flee **east toward Death's Valley**. Citywide chaos may let them slip away; exact means of losing the pursuit remain open.

## Koric entrusts the Tear to Veratus — October 15, 2026

During the escape, Moto is injured. Koric makes the hard decision to give the Tear to Veratus and says: **“If you don't find me tonight, find me in my next life. Only I can restore the Tear of Moana; it's my cross to bear.”** His physical separation, whether he expects to die that night, and the farewell's relation to the eventual restoration remain open. In Koric's immediately previous life, **Axios wanted the Tear as Mount Yokai's crown jewel**, straining their relationship. An evil yokai's possible possession/influence over Axios remains speculation, not established fact.

## Split escape from Sapporo and Moto's tunnel ambush — October 15, 2026

Koric turns back for injured Moto despite Moto urging him to go. Veratus escapes alone from the guards by running across a moving river; lonely and discouraged, he misses Rootport and his aunt/uncle. Koric and Moto buy safe passage at an inn from Koric's connections, then take an underground tunnel. **Proposed:** while they think they are safe, Moto falls silent; Koric sees a blade in his chest and fights the attacker. Reunion timing remains unresolved: Phil also described Koric and Moto waking Veratus the next morning or two days later for an embrace, which conflicts with Moto dying in the tunnel before Koric finds Veratus. Do not merge these into a fixed order yet.
