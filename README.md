## Evidence is not all the same

- **Author-confirmed:** explicit current decisions by Phil.
- **Recovered written lore:** what a particular source actually says; not an automatic endorsement of every old detail.
- **Historical / alternate / discarded:** retained for development history, not silently blended into current canon.
- **Game mechanics / crossover:** not automatically literal novel-world history.
- **Unverified:** an earlier assistant assertion or ambiguous reference without recovered supporting evidence.

**Current timeline anchors:** *Moana's Tear* opens at the beginning of **Age 4**. Humans do not inhabit Hokkaido during the novels; their arrival from the distant west is much later, **approximately Age 6**. The older Drive outline puts the Tear's return in Age 3; that discrepancy remains recorded rather than silently repaired.

The Google Drive originals were read, not edited. This repository contains organized research and source links, not a complete binary backup of the Drive or all its illustrations. Research date: October 11, 2026, author's local date.
