## Official planet name

- **Libriam** (L-I-B-R-I-A-M) is the **official name of the entire planet** where the Dragex books and Critz games are set; **Hokkaido is one of its three great continents**, not the name of the planet.
- **Libriam** is the author's coined name. An earlier AI response proposed a Latin-*libra* / balance interpretation. That is an *optional thematic association*, **not a verified literal Latin translation or a fixed in-universe etymology**.

## The world and its Creator

- **Allohim** creates the entire universe, including multiple galaxies and the creative beings assigned to design individual worlds.
- Different worlds are assigned different numbers of creative beings. **Earth**, in the **Milky Way**, is the *one world Allohim designed personally*; it is unimaginably distant and isolated from the other worlds, which will never contact it.
- **Dragex and Moana** are creative beings Allohim created and charged with designing the world of Libriam; they make the things within it. The exact distribution of their creations beyond earlier draft accounts is not newly specified.
- Hokkaido is the principal continent, shaped approximately like real Hokkaido, with several nearby islands, on a much smaller planet. The author's estimate is that Hokkaido covers **about 5% of the planet's total surface**. There is **no Japan-like continent south of Hokkaido**.
- **Two other major continents** on the same planet are discovered in **Age 4**. Critters who venture to them do not return; the reasons are unknown. Birds (the **hosts of heaven**) travel between these lands, and some great sea creatures have seen them. No names, coordinates, settlement histories, or reasons for non-return have been established.

## Time and transitions

1. **Creation:** starts with creation by Allohim; ends exactly as **Dragex fully falls asleep under Mount Yokai, preserved in obsidian**.
2. **Fall:** starts at that instant. Allohim raises the **Fey** as stewards; they seek power, split into rival factions despite being family, and fight. The death of the **last Fey still fighting at Mount Yokai** marks the end of Age 2 and beginning of Age 3; this is **not the global extinction of the Fey**. The battle devastates Fey civilization, but some remain elsewhere. The mountain is soaked in Fey blood.
3. **Redemptive Opportunity:** the age of the events in the **Dragex book series**, including Koric and Veratus's story, *Moana's Tear*. It ends **when Koric returns Moana's Tear**. By completing the return he is **finally able to die**; the timing and manner of his eventual death are not specified.
4. **Arcane:** begins with **Veratus reigning over the Kingdom of Moana**, the fertile kingdom restored in the former Death's Valley. Arcane technology advances drastically, including warfare. Other continents are discovered. It ends with the catastrophic **arcane wars** and their nuclear-like devastation.
5. **Fallout:** begins after those wars; ruined Hokkaido, scarce survivors, awful mutations, diseases, zombies and decay. Fey blood weakens with generations, causing critters gradually to revert toward ordinary animals. It ends **when Allohim judges and cleanses Hokkaido**.
6. **Man:** starts after that judgement. Allohim cleanses Hokkaido with fire, submerges it in the sea, and raises it cleansed. He plants a **garden on Eden's Isle** and places a **man there**, then gives him a **woman**; humanity expands from Eden's Isle. **Mankind (men and women)** is commissioned to **fill and subdue all of Libriam**, not merely Hokkaido. Humans stumble and fail but are repeatedly helped by Allohim, without whom they cannot complete the mission. Their opponents include grotesquely mutated creatures left by Fallout and **ancient Fey**, whose survival is consistent with the Fey population persisting beyond the Mount Yokai battle. This age ends when mankind, with Allohim's help, defeats the last enemy on Libriam, completes its conquest and subduing of all three continents, and defeats sin and death.
7. **Peace / Perfection:** confirmed eternal final age. Begins **the instant the last enemy is defeated on Libriam** following humanity's conquest and subduing of **all three continents**, with Allohim's indispensable help. **Sin and death are defeated**; **every creature on Libriam submits to Allohim, knows him, and willingly does his will**. Allohim's **Kingdom descends to Libriam**, establishing perfect balance and everlasting peace under mankind's rule, secured by Allohim's promise that sin can never return. Humanity subsequently harnesses Libriam's resources to explore other planets, help their inhabitants come to know and obey Allohim, and aid their struggles against sin. No eighth age follows.
