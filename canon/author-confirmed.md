# Author-confirmed canon — 2026-10-11

**Status:** Current direct author statements, superseding conflicting interpretations and older recovered documents. Review [the seven ages](../timeline/seven-ages.md), [cosmology](../world/allohim-and-cosmic-creation.md) and [narrative frame](../books/moanas-tear-frame.md).

## The world and its Creator

- **Allohim** creates the entire universe, including multiple galaxies and the creative beings assigned to design individual worlds.
- Different worlds are assigned different numbers of creative beings. **Earth**, in the **Milky Way**, is the *one world Allohim designed personally*; it is unimaginably distant and isolated from the other worlds, which will never contact it.
- **Dragex and Moana** are creative beings Allohim created and charged with designing the world of Hokkaido; they make the things within it. The exact distribution of their creations beyond earlier draft accounts is not newly specified.
- Hokkaido is the principal landmass, shaped approximately like real Hokkaido, with several nearby islands, on a much smaller planet. The author's estimate is that Hokkaido covers **about 5% of the planet's total surface**. There is **no Japan-like landmass south of Hokkaido**.
- **Two other major landmasses** on the same planet are discovered in **Age 4**. Critters who venture to them do not return; the reasons are unknown. Birds (the **hosts of heaven**) travel between these lands, and some great sea creatures have seen them. No names, coordinates, settlement histories, or reasons for non-return have been established.

## Time and transitions

1. **Creation:** starts with creation by Allohim; ends exactly as **Dragex fully falls asleep under Mount Yokai, preserved in obsidian**.
2. **Fall:** starts at that instant. Allohim raises the **Fey** as stewards; they seek power, split into rival factions despite being family, and fight. The **last Fey's death** at the **Battle of Mount Yokai** ends Age 2 and starts Age 3. The mountain is soaked in Fey blood.
3. **Redemptive Opportunity:** the age of the events in the **Dragex book series**, including Koric and Veratus's story, *Moana's Tear*. It ends **when Koric returns Moana's Tear**. By completing the return he is **finally able to die**; the timing and manner of his eventual death are not specified.
4. **Arcane:** begins with **Veratus reigning over the Kingdom of Moana**, the fertile kingdom restored in the former Death's Valley. Arcane technology advances drastically, including warfare. Other landmasses are discovered. It ends with the catastrophic **arcane wars** and their nuclear-like devastation.
5. **Fallout:** begins after those wars; ruined Hokkaido, scarce survivors, awful mutations, diseases, zombies and decay. Fey blood weakens with generations, causing critters gradually to revert toward ordinary animals. It ends **when Allohim judges and cleanses Hokkaido**.
6. **Man:** starts after that judgement. Allohim cleanses Hokkaido with fire, submerges it in the sea, and raises it cleansed. He plants a **garden on Eden's Isle** and places a **man there**, then gives him a **woman**; a growing human family expands from Eden's Isle across Hokkaido. Humans are imperfect and make mistakes, but on the whole they do reasonably well as the new stewards. This age is broadly peaceful. Its ending is not yet defined.
7. **Peace:** name retained from older outline as **provisional**. Its start, definitive character and exact difference from Age 6 remain **open**.

## Book 1, its frame, and characters

- **Moana's Tear** is book one. Its narrated adventure occurs **in Age 3**; the work is **written by Archaíos in Age 4**, as he approaches death.
- **Koric** (fire salamander) and **Veratus** (basilisk lizard) travel to return the Tear to the **center of Death's Valley**. The return brings back rain and life.
- The restored area becomes the fertile **Kingdom of Moana**, with **Veratus as its ruler at the beginning of Age 4**.
- **Archaíos** (author-confirmed spelling, including accent) is a very ancient tortoise and **first-generation Fey-blooded critter**, having personally drunk from the blood-tainted river following the Fey's fall. He dwells on **Eden's Isle**, west of Hokkaido.
- He sends letters **once per year** with the help of **migrating birds** passing the island. He knows he has **only one more annual opportunity** to send letters to the mainland before he dies, and chooses to write to his very distant female descendant **Olivia**, whom he calls **Olive**. The number of generations between them is not yet established; the author's “great-great-very great granddaughter” is deliberately non-numeric.
- **Olivia is 19** and wants to become a historian. She studies at the famous **school and Great Library in the Grandfather Tree, in the middle of Grimroot Grove**, under a legendary owl. Her exact relation to **Lady Wisdom** from older draft material has not been explicitly reconfirmed.
- It is **not established** whether the book comprises the text of a single letter, multiple letters, a chronicle accompanying his letters, or another presentation format. Do not invent the literary structure.

## Humans — latest resolution

- **No humans live in Hokkaido, and none are known there, during the Dragex novels (Age 3).**
- **Allohim introduces the first Hokkaido humans directly on Eden's Isle at the beginning of Age 6**. This replaces earlier assistant interpretation that the humans first arrive from a distant western continent by ordinary seafaring migration.
- An earlier October 11 statement suggested western arrival around Age 6; its relationship to this now-detailed Eden's Isle origin (if any) is **not resolved**. Do not invent a second wave or an origin on the western continent to reconcile it.
