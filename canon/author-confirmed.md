# Author-confirmed canon

**Status:** Current explicit author decisions. **Source:** Phil's October 11, 2026 conversation establishing this repository and supplying the Hokkaido maps. This page does not promote the entire recovered Drive archive to canon.

## Project identity

- **Dragex** is the book series: epic fantasy animal xenofiction.
- **Critz** is the associated game family sharing the universe.
- `Critz-Dragex` is intended to organize the lore so other agents can consult it without inventing continuity.
- The history is divided into **seven ages**.

## Book one

- Title: **Moana's Tear**.
- **Koric** (K-O-R-I-C) is a **fire salamander**.
- **Veratus** (V-E-R-A-T-U-S) is a **basilisk lizard**.
- Koric and Veratus journey together to return **Moana's Tear** to the **center of Death's Valley**.
- Returning the Tear brings **rain** back and allows life to flourish again.
- This return is the book's overarching final objective/outcome.
- The book **starts at the beginning of the Fourth Age**.

## Hokkaido and the wider world

- **Hokkaido is the landmass**, not an established name for the entire planet.
- Its outline and approximate size closely resemble real-world Hokkaido. There are a few surrounding islands.
- There is **no Japan-like landmass to the south**. Do not copy Honshu or the rest of Japan from a geographic reference into the fantasy world.
- The planet is much smaller than Earth. Phil's approximate scale is that Hokkaido covers **5% of the world's total surface**, not merely 5% of its land area.
- That percentage is a worldbuilding estimate, not a finalized planetary physics model. No planet name, diameter, gravity, or ocean-crossing time has been fixed here.
- **No humans live in Hokkaido during the Dragex novels, and none are known to its inhabitants.** This is not a statement that humans do not exist elsewhere.
- Humans eventually come from a faraway land across the ocean **to the west**, much later, **approximately in the Sixth Age**.
