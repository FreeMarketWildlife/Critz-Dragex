# Author-confirmed canon — 2026-10-11

**Status:** Current direct author statements, superseding conflicting interpretations and older recovered documents. Review [the seven ages](../timeline/seven-ages.md), [cosmology](../world/allohim-and-cosmic-creation.md) and [narrative frame](../books/moanas-tear-frame.md).

## Official planet name

- **Libriam** (L-I-B-R-I-A-M) is the **official name of the entire planet** where the Dragex books and Critz games are set; **Hokkaido is one of its three great landmasses**, not the name of the planet.
- **Libriam** is the author's coined name. An earlier AI response proposed a Latin-*libra* / balance interpretation. That is an *optional thematic association*, **not a verified literal Latin translation or a fixed in-universe etymology**.

## The world and its Creator

- **Allohim** creates the entire universe, including multiple galaxies and the creative beings assigned to design individual worlds.
- Different worlds are assigned different numbers of creative beings. **Earth**, in the **Milky Way**, is the *one world Allohim designed personally*; it is unimaginably distant and isolated from the other worlds, which will never contact it.
- **Dragex and Moana** are creative beings Allohim created and charged with designing the world of Libriam; they make the things within it. The exact distribution of their creations beyond earlier draft accounts is not newly specified.
- Hokkaido is the principal landmass, shaped approximately like real Hokkaido, with several nearby islands, on a much smaller planet. The author's estimate is that Hokkaido covers **about 5% of the planet's total surface**. There is **no Japan-like landmass south of Hokkaido**.
- **Two other major landmasses** on the same planet are discovered in **Age 4**. Critters who venture to them do not return; the reasons are unknown. Birds (the **hosts of heaven**) travel between these lands, and some great sea creatures have seen them. No names, coordinates, settlement histories, or reasons for non-return have been established.

## Time and transitions

1. **Creation:** starts with creation by Allohim; ends exactly as **Dragex fully falls asleep under Mount Yokai, preserved in obsidian**.
2. **Fall:** starts at that instant. Allohim raises the **Fey** as stewards; they seek power, split into rival factions despite being family, and fight. The death of the **last Fey still fighting at Mount Yokai** marks the end of Age 2 and beginning of Age 3; this is **not the global extinction of the Fey**. The battle devastates Fey civilization, but some remain elsewhere. The mountain is soaked in Fey blood.
3. **Redemptive Opportunity:** the age of the events in the **Dragex book series**, including Koric and Veratus's story, *Moana's Tear*. It ends **when Koric returns Moana's Tear**. By completing the return he is **finally able to die**; the timing and manner of his eventual death are not specified.
4. **Arcane:** begins with **Veratus reigning over the Kingdom of Moana**, the fertile kingdom restored in the former Death's Valley. Arcane technology advances drastically, including warfare. Other landmasses are discovered. It ends with the catastrophic **arcane wars** and their nuclear-like devastation.
5. **Fallout:** begins after those wars; ruined Hokkaido, scarce survivors, awful mutations, diseases, zombies and decay. Fey blood weakens with generations, causing critters gradually to revert toward ordinary animals. It ends **when Allohim judges and cleanses Hokkaido**.
6. **Man:** starts after that judgement. Allohim cleanses Hokkaido with fire, submerges it in the sea, and raises it cleansed. He plants a **garden on Eden's Isle** and places a **man there**, then gives him a **woman**; humanity expands from Eden's Isle. **Mankind (men and women)** is commissioned to **fill and subdue all of Libriam**, not merely Hokkaido. Humans stumble and fail but are repeatedly helped by Allohim, without whom they cannot complete the mission. Their opponents include grotesquely mutated creatures left by Fallout and **ancient Fey**, whose survival is consistent with the Fey population persisting beyond the Mount Yokai battle. This age ends when mankind, with Allohim's help, defeats the last enemy on Libriam, completes its conquest and subduing of all three landmasses, and defeats sin and death.
7. **Peace / Perfection:** confirmed eternal final age. Begins **the instant the last enemy is defeated on Libriam** following humanity's conquest and subduing of **all three landmasses**, with Allohim's indispensable help. **Sin and death are defeated**; **every creature on Libriam submits to Allohim, knows him, and willingly does his will**. Allohim's **Kingdom descends to Libriam**, establishing perfect balance and everlasting peace under mankind's rule, secured by Allohim's promise that sin can never return. Humanity subsequently harnesses Libriam's resources to explore other planets, help their inhabitants come to know and obey Allohim, and aid their struggles against sin. No eighth age follows.

## Book 1, its frame, and characters

- **Moana's Tear** is book one. Its narrated adventure occurs **in Age 3**; the work is **written by Archaíos in Age 4**, as he approaches death.
- **Koric** (fire salamander) and **Veratus** (basilisk lizard) travel to return the Tear to the **center of Death's Valley**. The return brings back rain and life.
- The restored area becomes the fertile **Kingdom of Moana**, with **Veratus as its ruler at the beginning of Age 4**.
- **Archaíos** (author-confirmed spelling, including accent) is a very ancient tortoise and **first-generation Fey-blooded critter**, having personally drunk from the blood-tainted river following the Fey's fall. He dwells on **Eden's Isle**, west of Hokkaido.
- He sends letters **once per year** with the help of **migrating birds** passing the island. He knows he has **only one more annual opportunity** to send letters to the mainland before he dies, and chooses to write to his very distant female descendant **Olivia**, whom he calls **Olive**. The number of generations between them is not yet established; the author's “great-great-very great granddaughter” is deliberately non-numeric.
- **Olivia is 19** and wants to become a historian. She studies at the famous **school and Great Library in the Grandfather Tree, in the middle of Grimroot Grove**, under a legendary owl. Her exact relation to **Lady Wisdom** from older draft material has not been explicitly reconfirmed.
- It is **not established** whether the book comprises the text of a single letter, multiple letters, a chronicle accompanying his letters, or another presentation format. Do not invent the literary structure.

## Humans — latest resolution

- **No humans live in Hokkaido, and none are known there, during the Dragex novels (Age 3).**
- **Allohim introduces the first Hokkaido humans directly on Eden's Isle at the beginning of Age 6**. This replaces earlier assistant interpretation that the humans first arrive from a distant western continent by ordinary seafaring migration.
- An earlier October 11 statement suggested western arrival around Age 6; its relationship to this now-detailed Eden's Isle origin (if any) is **not resolved**. Do not invent a second wave or an origin on the western continent to reconcile it.

## Fey survival and the end of Age 2 — clarification, October 12, 2026

- The description of **every Fey dying** at the Battle of Mount Yokai was **hyperbole for the catastrophic destruction of their civilization**, *not literal worldwide extinction*.
- The overwhelming majority of Fey—illustratively **about 99.99%**, not a verified census—took part in the battle. Not all participants necessarily died either; statements that “every Fey at the battle died” may themselves be hyperbolic.
- **Some Fey abstained from the battle**, including **newborn/young Fey** who were not there; **Fey also existed on the other two great landmasses of Libriam**.
- For chronological purposes, **Age 2 ends at the death of the last Fey fighting on Mount Yokai**, marking the end of the battle, collapse of the old Fey civilization, and start of **Age 3**. This marker should not be read as the death of the last Fey alive anywhere in Libriam.
- **Ancient Fey can therefore remain into later ages**, including as adversaries during the Age of Man, **without requiring an invented resurrection or retcon**. Specific individuals, numbers, lineages, survivorship, motives, and how long they live still need author decisions.

## Exact planetary scale — author decision, October 12, 2026

- The author specifies **Hokkaido at 83,422.22 km²**, roughly its real-world size, and **5% of Libriam’s total surface**. The resulting specified total surface is **1,668,444.4 km²**. This makes the previous approximate 5% relationship exact for the Dragex Globe model.
- Assuming a **spherical planet**, these inputs imply a **364.376984409 km radius**, **728.753968818 km diameter**, and **2,289.448114713 km circumference**. These are mathematical derivations, not independent measurements or claims about mass, gravity, atmosphere or habitability.
- The author requested a **Dragex Globe** program, with future expansion to designing Libriam’s solar system. Its globe placement, illustrated terrain, new location pins and celestial-body designs are proposals until explicitly confirmed. No star or planetary orbit is established by this request.
