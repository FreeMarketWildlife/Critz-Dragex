# Moana's Tear

**Series:** Dragex  
**Position:** Book 1  
**Status:** Foundational story premise — canon facts recorded; details still in development.

## Established canon

- The first book in the Dragex series is titled **Moana's Tear**.
- **Koric** (K-O-R-I-C) is a **fire salamander** and a principal character.
- **Veratus** (V-E-R-A-T-U-S) is a **basilisk lizard** who journeys with Koric.
- Their adventure involves returning **Moana's Tear** to the **center of Death's Valley**.
- Returning Moana's Tear will bring **rain** back to Death's Valley and allow **life to flourish there again**.
- The return of Moana's Tear is the book's overarching climactic objective / final outcome.

## Open questions — not established canon

- What exactly is Moana's Tear, and how did it leave Death's Valley?
- Why is Death's Valley deprived of rain and life?
- What motivates Koric and Veratus to undertake the journey?
- What is the geography of Death's Valley and the surrounding world?
- What happens along their journey?
- How does this story relate to the creation account and wider history of Dragex?
