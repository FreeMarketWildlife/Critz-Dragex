# Moana's Tear

**Series:** Dragex  
**Position:** Book 1  
**Status:** Author-confirmed foundational premise; detailed outline remains in development.  
**Timeline:** Begins at the **start of the Fourth Age**.

## Established canon

**Koric**, a fire salamander, and **Veratus**, a basilisk lizard, journey together to return **Moana's Tear** to the **center of Death's Valley**. Returning the Tear restores rainfall and allows life to flourish again. The return is the book's overarching final objective/outcome.

Source: Phil's explicit October 11, 2026 statements. Names were spelled by the author: K-O-R-I-C and V-E-R-A-T-U-S.

## Recovered development material

The Drive contains several incompatible openings, older political plots, a 2022 guild-bargain outline, an AI-assisted sixteen-chapter 2023 draft, a 2024 opening, and a May 2026 opening. They are documented separately in [manuscript versions](../research/drive-2026-10-11/manuscript-versions.md).

The sixteen-chapter 2023 draft ends with the surviving group **setting out for Death's Valley**, not with the actual return of the Tear. It must not be mistaken for a completed manuscript of the currently intended ending.

The April 2026 `[Critz] Lore` document puts the Tear's return in Age 3. This is a historical-source conflict with the author's current Age-4 opening; see [seven ages](../timeline/seven-ages.md). Do not silently move the novel or redefine an age to resolve it.
