# Narrative frame — Archaíos to Olivia

**Status:** Author-confirmed framework, with intentionally unresolved manuscript mechanics. **Source:** Author's October 11, 2026 account.

## Two timelines

- **Third Age / Redemptive Opportunity:** the events of *Moana's Tear*, Koric and Veratus's expedition, the return of the Tear, and the transition to a restored Kingdom of Moana.
- **Fourth Age / Arcane:** **Archaíos** writes the story while near the end of his life; **Olivia** receives it during the era of rising arcane technology.

## Archaíos

- Official author-confirmed spelling: **Archaíos** (accented í). This settles the current spelling for this character. Older **Archaois**, **Archaios**, and provisional **Archeios** remain searchable in research history.
- A very old tortoise on **Eden's Isle**, an island **west of Hokkaido**.
- **First-generation Fey-blooded:** he **personally drank from the river carrying Fey blood** after the last Fey still fighting died at the Battle of Mount Yokai. This does not make him one of the original Fey; it describes his generation of transformed animals.
- He knows he is dying and expects only **one more annual outgoing correspondence opportunity**.
- Birds migrating past Eden's Isle carry his letters to the mainland **once a year**.
- He chooses to write to his extremely distant granddaughter/descendant Olivia about the historical events of *Moana's Tear*. Number and names of intervening generations are **not determined**.

## Olivia / Olive

- Official name **Olivia**, affectionate name **Olive** given by Archaíos.
- A **19-year-old** girl/descendant of Archaíos.
- Wants to become a **historian**.
- Attends **Hokkaido's most legendary historical school**, situated in the **Great Library of the Grandfather Tree**, in the middle of **Grimroot Grove**, a dark forest.
- **October 19, 2026 update:** **Naboo** runs the archive/library, having taken over shortly after Mount Yokai. He is a first-generation owl who drank from the River of Blood; “fey owl” means a Fey-blooded animal with humanlike qualities, not an original Fey being (author clarification, October 19). The spelling **feysian / feyjian** and any identity with **Lady Wisdom** remain unresolved. The school’s formal name is undecided. See [Grandfather Tree and Naboo](../world/grandfather-tree.md).
- Her **species**, immediate family, appearance, exact curriculum, and reactions to the letters are **not given**. Do not assume that she is a tortoise solely because she descends from Archaíos.

## Open literary decisions

Is the finished book the letter itself, a set of letters, a separate history included in the final mailing, or a third-person narrative presented within a letter? Does Olive read each part on receipt? Who narrates scenes Archaíos could not personally witness? How long after the Third Age did he write? How does the owl or school respond? Do not invent answers.
