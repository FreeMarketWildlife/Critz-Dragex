# Instructions for lore and writing agents

Phil is the author. This repository is a continuity and research system, not permission to invent canon.

## Reading order

Read `canon/author-confirmed.md`, `timeline/seven-ages.md`, `world/libriam.md`, `books/moanas-tear-frame.md`, `world/allohim-and-cosmic-creation.md`, and the relevant topic files. For recovered details, consult `research/drive-2026-10-11/sources.md` and the original source when the distinction matters. Read `research/drive-2026-10-11/conflicts.md` before resolving apparent contradictions.

## Source precedence

1. Phil's latest explicit decision governs over an older draft.
2. A source-backed entry states what that source says, not necessarily the current truth of the universe.
3. Document modification dates are evidence about versions, not proof of canon status or fictional chronology.
4. AI-assisted drafts, brainstorming, discarded scenes, cosmetics, unlock trees, and game balance numbers must remain labeled as such.
5. Do not use an earlier assistant's unsourced recollection as primary evidence. The `research/legacy/` snapshots contain such material and are explicitly non-authoritative.
6. When Phil confirms a change, update the canonical entry and the affected timeline/game register, and record what it supersedes. Preserve historical evidence rather than rewriting its contents to match.

## Names and dictation

Official spellings explicitly given: **Dragex**, **Critz**, **Koric**, **Veratus**, **Moana's Tear**. Normalize obvious dictation variants of these in context. A near-match is not a new character or alternate canonical name. Ask before adopting an uncertain new name.

**Archaíos** is now explicitly author-confirmed (accented spelling); keep archival variants searchable: Archaois / Archaios / Archeios. Other unresolved variants: Psyte / Pyste; Kaldro's Cape / Kalos' Cape; Morain / Morane; Kurosu / Kuroso; Mount Yokai / Mount Yotei. Do not automatically merge Draco with Drago, Makani with Dragex, or Enáretos with the unverified Korok reference.

## Timeline discipline

- **There are seven ages**. Author's latest account puts **the events of Moana's Tear in Age 3** and **Archaíos's writing of the book in Age 4**. Age 3 ends with the Tear's return; Age 4 opens with Veratus's reign over the Kingdom of Moana. The earlier Age-4 novel event placement is superseded.
- **Judgement ends Age 5**; the Sixth Age is **Man**, with humans directly placed by Allohim on Eden's Isle. The older “Age of Judgement” label is superseded. **The Seventh Age is confirmed eternal Peace / Perfection:** it begins the instant the **last enemy** is defeated after humanity, helped by Allohim, has filled and subdued **all three continents** of **Libriam**.
- **Libriam is the official planet name; Hokkaido is a continent.** At the end of Age 6, sin and death are defeated on Libriam; Allohim's Kingdom descends; all creatures obey him. Age 7 is everlasting, but its people explore **other reachable planets** to help them fight sin. **Earth is excluded from contact** under the earlier cosmic rule. Do not casually introduce renewed sin on Libriam, space contact with Earth, or unsanctioned eighth ages.
- **Age 2 does not end in the extinction of Fey worldwide.** It ends when the last Fey fighting on Mount Yokai dies, as the civilization collapses. Statements that all the Fey (or even all combatants) died are rhetorical; overwhelming participation (approximately 99.99%) is illustrative, not a literal census. **Fey elsewhere on Libriam, abstainers, and young/newborn Fey survive.** Ancient Fey in Age 6 therefore require no invented resurrection, though their individual identities remain undefined.
- **No humans live in Hokkaido in the novels**. The first local humans are placed by Allohim on Eden's Isle in Age 6. A prior western-seafaring origin claim is not to be repeated as current canon; its relationship to the later account is unresolved.
- Do not assign games to ages from their genre, weapons, technology, boss appearances, or atmosphere. Phil explicitly reserved those assignments.
- A game can have a main setting and separate historical missions, flashbacks, or framing narration. Record those separately.
- Do not convert AC / BC / ADF calendar numbers or character ages into seven-age dates without a confirmed mapping.

## Cross-media boundaries

The original ten-game registry comes from the retrieved October 9 game-planning conversation; the October 19 update adds Critz: Treetop Trebuchet with an explicit early-Age-4 setting. Most older Drive game documents describe the original shooter. A Conquest match is not a new game title; the shooter's Duel mode is not automatically the standalone platform fighter. The 2025 `[Critz] Campaign` missions are not automatically the final scope of the 2026 standalone Campaign concept.

The Dragex tabletop rulebook and PowPass–Dragex collaboration have separate records. Their currencies, elemental icons, reward eggs, and mechanics do not establish novel-world biology or chronology. Reference images from other franchises are design inspirations, not inhabitants of Hokkaido.

## Writing and maintenance

Keep facts attributable to a source ID, a named version, or a dated author decision. Label inference as inference and proposals as proposals. Do not invent missing chapters, identities, genealogies, motivations, species, maps, or age boundaries. Do not import personal finance, credentials, contact information, or unrelated private material into this public repository. Verify a write before saying it was saved.
